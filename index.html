What this version can and cannot do

Included: procedural 3D character, simple environments, scene transitions, camera movement, captions, browser rendering and WebM export.

Not included yet: generative AI character art, advanced skeletal animation, automatic lip-sync, AI voice generation, photorealistic scenes, or guaranteed MP4 export. These need additional engines or services. This build prioritizes a genuinely free mobile prototype.
